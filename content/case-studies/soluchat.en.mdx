## Context

Soluchat is a real-time messaging application meant to replace an email
thread between project teams spread across several time zones.

## Constraints

- Perceived latency under 200ms on unstable connections.
- History browsable without reloading the entire conversation.
- No dependency on a third-party messaging service.

## Stack

React, TypeScript on the client. Backend in Rust, communication over
WebSocket.

## Decisions & trade-offs

The backend was written in **Rust** rather than Node: the learning cost was
real, but predictable memory management and the absence of GC pauses
mattered more on connections that were already fragile.

Client-side state stayed deliberately simple — no global state management
library. At this scale, one would have added indirection without solving a
real problem.

## What didn't work

The first version reloaded the entire history on every reconnection, which
made network drops very costly. Cursor-based pagination arrived in the
second iteration, after measuring the impact.

## Outcome

Messaging used daily by project teams, with browsable history and seamless
reconnection.
