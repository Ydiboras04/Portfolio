## Context

A Salesforce integrator generated its European clients' contractual
documents by hand from Salesforce: quotes, confirmations, contracts. Each
document required manual rework, and each rework introduced a risk of error
on paperwork that carries legal weight for the end client.

## Constraints

- Documents had to stay compliant with each end client's brand guidelines.
- No manual intervention between the Salesforce data and the signed PDF.
- Templates had to remain editable by non-developers.

## Stack

Salesforce (administration, profiles), PDF Butler, FORM Butler, SIGN Butler.

## Decisions & trade-offs

Data mapping was centralised rather than duplicated per template. It took
longer to set up, but a structural change on the Salesforce side no longer
breaks ten templates at once.

Templates stayed owned by the business teams. It would have been faster to
hard-code everything; that would have made the team dependent on a
developer for every comma.

## What didn't work

The first workflows failed silently when an expected field was empty on the
Salesforce side — the document was generated, incomplete, with no alert. The
fix was to validate the data upstream of generation rather than inspect the
PDFs afterwards.

## Outcome

End-to-end document generation, with no manual rework, for European
clients. Templates remain maintained by the business teams.
